Call of Duty

Call of Duty (COD) is a popular first-person shooter video game franchise published by Activision. The series features different historical and modern military settings, campaigns, multiplayer modes, and cooperative gameplay.

Since its debut in 2003, the franchise has expanded into multiple sub-series, introducing new characters, maps, weapons, and gameplay experiences.

History

2003 – The Beginning

The original Call of Duty was released in 2003. It focused on World War II and introduced players to cinematic military missions.

2007 – Modern Warfare

Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare introduced a modern military setting and became one of the franchise's most influential releases.

2010 – Black Ops

Call of Duty: Black Ops introduced a new storyline involving Cold War operations, espionage, and memorable characters.

2019 – Modern Warfare Reboot

Modern Warfare returned with updated graphics, gameplay mechanics, and a reimagined storyline.

Call of Duty: Mobile

Call of Duty: Mobile is a free-to-play mobile first-person shooter released in 2019. It brings familiar Call of Duty gameplay to Android and iOS devices.

Game Modes

  • Multiplayer
  • Battle Royale
  • Ranked Matches
  • Zombie Mode (when available)
  • Limited-Time Events

Features

Players can customize their loadouts, unlock operators, collect weapon skins, and compete in different multiplayer maps.

Weapons

Call of Duty features different weapon categories. Each category offers different gameplay styles.

  • Assault Rifles: Versatile weapons.
  • SMGs: Designed for close-range combat.
  • Sniper Rifles: Designed for long-range combat.
  • Shotguns: Powerful at close range.
  • LMGs: High ammunition capacity.
  • Pistols: Secondary weapons.

Gameplay

Gameplay varies depending on the title. Most games include campaign missions, multiplayer battles, and cooperative modes. Players can select different classes, equipment, and customization options.
